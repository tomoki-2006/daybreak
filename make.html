Every morning, your lock screen gets closer.

Pick a deadline, and Daybreak counts down the mornings, checks off milestones, and brightens the sky from night to sunrise.

  • Clear space up top, so the clock fits.
  • Five short milestones, in order, checked off as their dates pass.
  • A new picture for every morning, set by a Shortcut.
